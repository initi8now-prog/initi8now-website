Questions students ask us

Is Initi8Now free for students? +

Yes. There is no registration or placement fee, and there never will be. If anyone asks you for money in our name, report it to info@initi8now.com.

How do you verify employers? +

We confirm the company's legal identity, verify the hiring contact, review the role for clarity on hours and pay, and check payment terms before a listing goes live. Listings with red flags never reach you.

Which colleges can join? +

Any college in India. We started with IITs and NITs and are now onboarding students from universities and colleges across every state.

What kind of work is available? +

Part-time roles (content, social media, sales, tutoring, events, data work, campus ambassador programs), paid internships in tech, design and business, and short project-based gigs.

When do I get app access? +

We're rolling out early access in batches. Fill the 20-second form and you'll get job drops by email now, and the app as soon as your batch opens.
