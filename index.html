    Questions students ask us

    Is Initi8Now free for students? +

    Yes. There is no registration or placement fee, and there never will be. If anyone asks you for money in our name, report it to info@initi8now.com.

    How do you verify employers? +

    We confirm the company’s legal identity, verify the hiring contact, review the role for clarity on hours and pay, and check payment terms before a listing goes live. Listings with red flags never reach you.

    Can students from any college join? +

    Yes, any college in India. We started with IITs and NITs and are now onboarding students from universities and colleges across every state.

    What kind of work will be available? +

    Part-time roles such as content, social media, sales, tutoring, events, data work and campus ambassador programmes; paid internships in tech, design and business; and short project-based gigs.

    When do I get app access? +

    We are rolling out early access in batches. Fill in the 20-second form and you will get job drops by email now, and the app as soon as your batch opens.
