For students.

Is it really free? +

Yes. There is no registration fee, no application fee and no placement fee. If anyone asks you for money in our name, report it to info@initi8now.com straight away.

Who can join? +

Any student aged 18 or over, enrolled at a recognised Indian institution. We started with IITs and NITs and are onboarding colleges across every state.

How do you verify employers? +

Five checks: business identity, a human reading the listing, escrow funding, a location and safety check, and post-gig scoring. A listing that fails any step never goes live. The safety page sets out each one.

When and how do I get paid? +

The employer funds escrow before applications open. Once your work is approved, the payout reaches your linked UPI account within 24 hours.

What if the employer refuses to pay? +

The money is already held in escrow, so it is not theirs to withhold. A disputed delivery goes to review, and escrow is released, split or returned based on your logged hours and the evidence.

What are skill badges? +

Verified marks on your profile earned by finishing real gigs. Unlike a CV line, an employer can see where each one came from.

Will this clash with my classes? +

You set the hours you are free and matching respects them. Late-night and isolated-site work is not accepted on the platform at all.

When do I get app access? +

Early access rolls out in batches, college by college. Fill in the 20-second form and you will get verified job drops by email in the meantime.

For employers.

How long does it take to post a role? +

About five minutes to write it, and one working day for us to verify your company before it goes live.

What will you ask me for? +

CIN/GST or equivalent, a company-domain email for the hiring contact, clear role terms, and your payment terms. Listings with unpaid “trial” work are declined.

What does it cost? +

Listing is free. A flat platform fee applies to released escrow at public launch, and it is 0% during early access. See pricing.

What if a student does not deliver? +

Escrow is only released on your approval, or through our dispute process. We will also find a replacement.

For colleges.

What does a campus partnership involve? +

Your placement cell gets a live view of student earnings and skills, an alumni-to-student referral bridge, and campus-only boards. It is free for partner institutions.

Do you share individual student data with us? +

Only in aggregate, unless a student explicitly consents to share their own record. That rule is written into our privacy policy.

How do we start? +

Email info@initi8now.com or use the contact form and choose “Campus partnership”.
