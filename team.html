Why "Initi8"

Initiate, integrity, implement. The three I's the company runs on — and the 8 in the middle is an infinity loop, because every completed gig should open the door to the next one. That's the promise behind the name and behind the badge system.

How we work

Trust first

We'd rather list fewer roles than list one we can't vouch for. Verification is the product.

Students, not leads

No selling student data, no pay-to-apply. If it wouldn't be fair to a younger sibling, we don't do it.

Skills compound

Every gig should leave a student more employable than before. Badges make that visible.
