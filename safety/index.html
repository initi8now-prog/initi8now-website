Verification pipeline

Five checks before a gig reaches you.

Order matters here — each check gates the next. A listing that fails any step never goes live.

  • 1
    Business identityCIN/GST or registration proof, plus a working business domain. Individuals are verified with government ID.
  • 2
    Human review of the listingA person reads every gig. Vague scope, no stated rate, or “fees payable” language gets rejected.
  • 3
    Escrow fundingThe employer transfers the budget before applications open. No funds, no listing.
  • 4
    Location and safety checkOn-ground gigs are mapped to a verifiable address. Late-night and isolated-site work is not accepted.
  • 5
    Post-gig scoringStudents rate employers. Two credible complaints suspend the account pending review.
Know the signs

If you see this anywhere, it is a scam.

These apply to any platform, not only ours. Share them with a friend who is job hunting.

  • ✗
    A registration or training fee. Legitimate work never charges you to start.
  • ✗
    Payment “after the trial period” with no written scope.
  • ✗
    Chats moved to a personal number straight after the first message.
  • ✗
    Requests for your OTP, UPI PIN or bank login. Nobody legitimate needs these.
  • ✗
    Your ID document asked for over WhatsApp. KYC happens inside the app only.
  • ✗
    Urgency and countdowns. “Only three seats left” exists to stop you checking.

In danger right now? Call 112. For cyber fraud in India, report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
